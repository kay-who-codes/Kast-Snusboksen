How to play:
1. Everyone sits in a circle.
2. Read the question out loud.
3. Throw the snus tin to the person who fits the statement best.
4. The person who receives the tin throws it to start the next round.
Bad catch: Can't catch a good throw? Drink three sips! 🍺
Cheers: Every 10th question – everyone drinks together! 🥂
